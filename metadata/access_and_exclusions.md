# Public access and exclusions

The deposit contains the files listed in FILE_MANIFEST.csv only. Source material
was selected by allow-list rather than copied recursively from results/ or
postprocess_results/. Statistical files are network/group summaries.

Excluded classes:
- Ground discharge time series and original gauge measurements.
- Observation-level model, empirical and product evaluation tables, including
  tables containing observed discharge, predicted-observed pairs or row keys.
- Serialized caches (including data.pkl), raw station metadata and coordinates,
  machine-readable station ID lists, privately installed environments, downloaded satellite archives,
  raw river boundary files and user-specific authentication material.
- Manuscript DOCX files, tracked documents, reviewer comments/replies, line maps,
  response audits and document-preparation history.
- Native vector source for the observed-discharge scatter in Figure 3.

Published aggregate outputs and the final rendered figures are supplied to
support inspection of the paper. Full observation-level numerical reproduction
requires permissions and inputs beyond the public release. A synthetic/example
input, if provided by a script, is not an actual gauge record and cannot validate
the reported numerical results.

There is no public or private Zenodo record associated with this local package.
No file has been uploaded. A restricted Zenodo record would also require any
permissions needed to deposit or share provider data; setting a visibility flag
does not grant such permissions.
